ERFWeb · Feature overview02

Who uses it

Six roles share one workflow.

ROLE 01

Employee

Files reports, attaches receipts, re-files when returned.

ROLE 02

Signatory Level 1

Endorses the reports of the people they cover.

ROLE 03

Signatory Level 2

Gives final approval and decides Finance queries.

ROLE 04

Finance

Checks receipts, clears for payment, approves signatures.

ROLE 05

Expense Verifier

Checks one line that Finance has questioned.

ROLE 06

Administrator

Sets up accounts, rates and rules; never sees reports.

Approval · The inbox

Approvers decide from one inbox.

ActionWhoWhat happens
ApproveLevel 1, Level 2Moves to the next step; the last approval sends it to Finance
ReturnLevel 1, Level 2Back to the employee with a reason; approval restarts on re-filing
RejectLevel 1, Level 2Final, with a reason
OverrideLevel 2Approves past a Level 1 who has not acted, with a reason
Lower a lineLevel 1Reduces an amount before approving; the filed amount stays visible

The inbox shows my turn, waiting for an earlier step and done. Nobody approves their own report.

ERFWeb · Feature overview11

Approval · Routing

Nobody picks an approver by hand.

SCOPE

Coverage decides

Each signatory covers a department or named people. ERFWeb finds every employee’s Level 1 and Level 2.

FILING

Fixed when filed

The route is saved at filing, so later scope changes never re-route a report in progress.

DELEGATION

Stand-in, up to 90 days

An absent signatory names a stand-in. The R47 shows “on behalf of”.

ERFWeb · Feature overview15

Finance · Verification

A doubtful line gets an independent check.

01

Finance flags one line on an approved report and gives the reason.

02

An Expense Verifier not involved in that report picks it up.

03

The finding: Verified, Verified with a note, or Not verified.

04

If not verified, Finance refers it to the Level 2 approver.

05

Level 2 decides: let it through, return the report, or reject it.

Clearing waits until every open check is closed. Receipts can still be recorded meanwhile.
